How to use: Jacquard 12 native size 21 px (21, 42, 63 ...), Jacquard 24 native size 43 px (43, 86 ...); antialiasing off, hinting off. Measured: at any other size the pixels come out uneven. Blackletter capitals are hard to read in ALL CAPS ("GRAVE DEPTHS" is a struggle), so use Title Case ("Grave Depths"). Sample: ../_sheets/display_jacquard.png
