STYLE CHECK AGAINST THE PRIMARY PACK (honest)
  Same scale and shading as 0x72, but the outline colour is a dark maroon #1c1117 instead of 0x72's #222222
  (about 31% of this pack's opaque pixels). Side by side on a dark floor it reads as the same style; for a perfect
  match swap that one colour to #222222 when importing (the permission above allows modifying the sprites).
  Theme is woodland, so only the dungeon-suitable ones are worth using: Golem (64x64, boss), Troll (boss),
  Gnolls (4), Wolf, Bear, Bandit, Mushroom folk (3), Fairy (familiar), Ent.
