Place ambience loops and footsteps (added 12 Nov 2026, sound pass of the revamp)
All four sources are human recordings published on OpenGameArt years before generative audio
tools existed. Retrieved 2026-11-12 (UTC). The untouched downloads are in ../audio/ambience_src/,
with the script that made the game files (build_place_audio.sh: gain to a loudness target, a
1.2 to 1.5 s crossfade at the loop seam, re-encoding; nothing synthesised, no effects added).
