# Hollow Hill: full review against the reference games (14 Nov 2026, v0.39.0)

Compared side by side with store screenshots of: Octopath Traveler ($59.99, the owner's look
reference), Sea of Stars ($34.99), Final Fantasy I Pixel Remaster ($11.99) and Chained Echoes ($24.99).
Target: $5 to $10, and a publisher.

## Scores (1 to 10, against those games)
| Area | Score | One line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lighting and mood | 7 | Closest to the references: warm lamps, night, water, tilt-shift. |
| Environment art | 5 | Places are a few pieces spread on flat ground; the references are dense, layered, hand-built. |
| Characters and animation | 4 | Small sprites, no attack frames for most, no portraits with emotions. |
| Battle presentation | 5 | Clean and readable, but plain backgrounds and few, small effects. |
| Battle depth | 4 | Attack / skills / items / guard. No timing, combos, weaknesses or break system. |
| World map | 3 | The weakest: one sand plain with floating names; FF1 has a real readable map + minimap. |
| UI | 6 | Consistent and clean; big on the phone; no map screen, quest log, bestiary. |
| Story and writing | 5 | Good premise; AI-written first draft (must be rewritten or disclosed). |
| Content and length | 2 | About 1 to 2 hours, 2 of 4 chapters, 2 bosses. The target is 8 to 10 hours. |
| Music and sound | 4 | Free tracks, few of them, nobody has listened in game. No themes of its own. |
| Polish and testing | 3 | Never played start to end by a person; Windows build never started. |

Overall: a strong-looking vertical slice of chapter one; about 15 to 20% of a $10 game.

## What we need, most important first
1. Content: chapters 3 and 4, the ending, side quests, secrets, optional dungeons and bosses (8 to 10 h).
2. A real battle hook: one idea of our own (e.g. timed blocks/strikes or a break/weakness bar).
3. Dungeons: places with puzzles, keys, treasure chests and a boss each (none in the story today).
4. Bosses: one per land plus mini-bosses, each with a mechanic.
5. World map: a readable map with roads, rivers, bridges, mountains, a minimap and town icons.
6. Denser places: more props, layers, interiors you can enter, NPCs with routines.
7. Characters: portraits with expressions, attack animations, more monster variety per land.
8. Music: a main theme, town, field, battle, boss, dungeon tracks (commissioned or licensed, human-made).
9. Menus: map screen, quest log, bestiary, save slots, options for text speed.
10. A full playtest by people, bug fixing, a real Windows/Steam Deck test.

## For a publisher
They will want: a 20-30 min demo of the best quality, a 60-90 s trailer, a one-page pitch (hook, market,
price, scope, schedule, budget), proof the team can finish (a schedule and what is done), and an answer
on AI use (art and audio human-made; story text must be rewritten by the owner).
