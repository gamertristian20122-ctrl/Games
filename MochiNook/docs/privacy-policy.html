MochiNook Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

The short version: everything you put into MochiNook stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

What stays on your phone

Your routine, quests, checkmarks, onboarding answers (like your brain profile, energy level, and daily times), your pet, Pebbles, shop items, and settings are saved only in MochiNook’s private storage on your device.

We never receive this information. It is removed when you use “Delete all my data” in Settings or uninstall the app.

What leaves your phone

Purchases. If you buy MochiNook Pro or leave a tip, the payment is handled by Google Play. MochiNook uses RevenueCat to confirm purchases, which receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipts, and basic device information (such as the operating system version). RevenueCat’s privacy policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Google’s is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Nothing else. MochiNook has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting service, and no tracking.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. There is no push-notification server, and you can turn reminders off any time in Settings.

Caregiver share codes

A share code contains your routine and, if you choose, your daily times. It is created on your phone and only goes wherever you decide to send it. We never see it.

Calm picks

The small card at the bottom of the home screen is static text built into the app. It contains no links, tracking, or ad network, and Pro hides it.

Children and teens

MochiNook is designed to be safe for teens. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, and it has no chat or contact with other users.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete all my data. This erases everything MochiNook saved on your phone and cancels its reminders. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription. You can cancel MochiNook Pro in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.

Questions? Use the developer email shown on MochiNook’s Google Play page.
