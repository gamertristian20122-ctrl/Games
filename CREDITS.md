## How this game was made

- **Art, fonts, sound and music:** made by people (Kenney and Juhani Junkala). Nothing
  here is AI-generated.
- **Code and in-game text** (item names, descriptions, menus): written with an AI coding
  assistant, directed and reviewed by the developer. All of the text lives in plain files
  (`scripts/data/`, `scripts/ui/`) and can be rewritten by hand at any time.

Steam asks about AI use when a game is submitted. As reported in January 2026, its form
asks about AI-made content that **players see or hear**, and no longer about AI help with
code. Under that wording the AI-written item text would need to be disclosed unless you
rewrite it in your own words first. Rules change, so read the form itself when you get
there and answer it from this section.
